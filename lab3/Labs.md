1. Build an AI Startup from Scratch
Students must use prompt engineering to build a complete startup idea using AI within one hour. Teams should generate a startup concept, identify the target audience, define the problem statement, create features, generate a business model, and even design a pitch tagline. They should refine prompts multiple times to improve the quality and creativity of AI responses. At the end, each team presents their startup idea along with the prompts they used and explains how prompt improvements changed the output quality.
Expected Deliverables
●	Startup Name
●	Problem Statement
●	Target Audience
●	Core Features
●	Revenue Model
●	Marketing Tagline
●	Final Optimized Prompt

2. Reverse Engineering AI Challenge
Teams must choose a popular app, platform, or digital product and deeply analyze how its core features might actually work behind the scenes. The goal is not to redesign the app visually, but to use prompt engineering to break down the product like real engineers, product managers, and AI architects. Students must guide AI step-by-step to uncover system design, workflows, recommendation engines, monetization strategies, user psychology, databases, APIs, AI usage, and scalability approaches used in the product.
Students are not allowed to ask AI generic questions like “How does Instagram work?”. Instead, they must progressively refine prompts to extract deeper technical and business insights. The challenge is designed to test reasoning, decomposition, curiosity, and advanced prompting skills rather than simple AI content generation.
Suggested Products
●	Instagram Reels
●	WhatsApp
●	Swiggy
●	Netflix
●	Spotify
●	Amazon
●	Uber
●	ChatGPT
●	YouTube
Expected Deliverables
●	Product Breakdown
●	Core Features Analysis
●	Backend/System Workflow
●	Recommendation or AI Logic
●	User Retention Strategies
●	Revenue Generation Model
●	Scalability Challenges
●	Feature Improvement Suggestions
●	Prompt Iteration Journey
●	Final “Master Prompt” Used by the Team

3. AI Problem Solver Challenge
Each team receives a real-world problem related to students, education, cities, healthcare, environment, or productivity. Using only prompt engineering and AI tools, they must develop a practical solution. Students should prompt AI to generate workflows, automation ideas, implementation strategies, business models, and awareness campaigns. The focus is on problem-solving using structured prompting rather than coding.
Example Problems
●	Reducing hostel food waste
●	Improving classroom attendance
●	Smart transport for students
●	Mental health support for teenagers
●	AI-based study planner
Expected Deliverables
●	Problem Analysis
●	AI-Based Solution
●	Workflow Diagram
●	Features
●	Awareness or Marketing Plan
●	Final Optimized Prompt

4. AI Content Creator Battle
Students must use prompt engineering to create a complete social media campaign for a brand, product, or college event. Teams should generate Instagram posts, captions, ad copies, YouTube video ideas, thumbnails, hooks, and LinkedIn posts using AI. The challenge teaches students how prompting affects creativity, branding, and audience engagement.

●	Instagram Post Ideas
●	Viral Hooks
●	Ad Copy
●	YouTube Video Titles
●	Thumbnail Text Ideas
●	LinkedIn Announcement Post

5. AI Teacher & Learning Assistant Challenge
Teams must transform AI into the best teacher for a difficult topic using advanced prompting techniques. They should make AI explain concepts in simple language, generate quizzes, provide analogies, create learning roadmaps, and conduct mock interviews or viva sessions. The goal is to engineer prompts that make AI highly interactive, engaging, and personalized for learning.
Suggested Topics
●	Artificial Intelligence
●	Cybersecurity
●	Blockchain
●	Data Science
●	Physics or Mathematics
●	Programming Basics
Expected Deliverables
●	Topic Explanation
●	Beginner-Friendly Notes
●	Quiz Questions
●	Real-World Analogies
●	Learning Roadmap
●	Mock Interview Questions
●	Final Master Prompt
